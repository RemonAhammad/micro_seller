Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Black Shadow Software Ltd ("we", "our", "us") built the MicroSeller app. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

MicroSeller is designed with privacy in mind. All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers.

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Product catalog (names, SKUs, prices, stock levels, variants, images)
  • Orders and order history (items, amounts, payment status, delivery info)
  • Customer information (names, phone numbers, addresses, purchase history)
  • Supplier information (names, contact details, purchase records)
  • Expense records (amounts, categories, dates)
  • Stock movements and inventory adjustments
  • Cash register sessions (opening/closing amounts)
  • App preferences (theme, language, currency, tax rate, shop name)

Google Drive Backup (Optional)

If you choose to connect your Google account, the app can backup your business data to your personal Google Drive. This feature is entirely optional.

  • We use Google Sign-In to authenticate your Google account.
  • We request access only to the app-specific folder on your Google Drive (driveAppdataScope). We cannot access any other files on your Drive.
  • The backup contains your business data in JSON format.
  • A maximum of 5 backups are kept; older ones are automatically cleaned up.
  • Your Google email address is displayed in the app solely to show which account is connected.
  • We do not store your Google credentials. Authentication is handled entirely by Google's OAuth 2.0 system.

Local Backup (Optional)

You can export your data as a JSON file to your device storage and share it via any method (email, messaging, file transfer). This backup file is created and stored entirely on your device. We have no access to it.

Camera Permission

The app requests camera access solely for scanning QR codes and barcodes on products. No photos or camera data are transmitted or stored beyond the scanned code value.

Photo/Storage Permission

The app requests photo/storage access solely for picking product images from your gallery. Images are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties. Your data stays on your device and, if you opt in, on your personal Google Drive.

Analytics and Tracking

MicroSeller does not include any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not collect usage statistics or behavioral data.

Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally using an embedded database (Isar) on your device's secure storage.
  • Google Drive backups are protected by your Google account security.
  • We recommend keeping your device locked with a PIN, password, or biometric authentication.

Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete individual records (products, orders, customers, expenses, etc.) from within the app.
  • Clear all data from Settings > Clear Data.
  • Disconnect Google Drive and delete backups from Settings > Google Drive Backup.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

MicroSeller is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
